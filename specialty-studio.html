02 — The Ritual

Unhurried, by design.

  1. I.

    The Consultation

    We begin with a conversation: your routine, your hair’s history and the shape you’re imagining. Bring references if you have them, or let us propose one.

  2. II.

    The Reading

    Density, growth patterns, cowlicks and texture are studied before a single cut, so the shape works with your hair rather than against it.

  3. III.

    The Cut

    Sectioned, measured and refined in stages. Every line is checked against your face, your profile and the way you wear your hair.

  4. IV.

    The Finish

    Styled, then explained: how to wear it day to day, which products it needs, and how it will grow out over the weeks ahead.

The Standard

What every cut promises.

Ⅰ
