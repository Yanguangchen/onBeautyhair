The On Story

From Bukit Merah To Three Neighbourhood Salons

On Hair Salon & Beauty grew from its Bukit Merah roots into three outlets across Singapore: Jalan Bukit Merah, Bukit Merah Central and Bedok North. Each outlet is here for everyday grooming, important occasions and the small confidence boost that comes from feeling properly looked after.

Our work begins before the service starts. We ask what you want, look at what your hair, skin or nails need, and explain the practical options before making a recommendation. The aim is simple: a result that looks good, feels right and is realistic to maintain.

Whether you are visiting for a trim, color work, nails, makeup or bridal preparation, you can expect clear advice, careful finishing and a team that does not rush you into services you do not need.
