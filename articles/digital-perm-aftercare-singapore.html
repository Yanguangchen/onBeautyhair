Hair guide · 25 October 2026

After a digital perm in Singapore humidity

A digital perm is the curl option on the On Hair Salon menu, not the straightening one. Digital perm, cut and treatment is listed from S$98 to S$298+. The final figure is confirmed after a consultation, the same way other “+” prices on the site are. This note is about the days after you leave, which the comparison guide does not walk through on its own.

Ask before you stand up

The stylist who did the perm is the person who knows the product on your hair that day. Before you leave, ask three things: when to wash, what to skip on the lengths, and how they want you to dry it in this weather. Do not replace that answer with a number from another salon. Overlapping a fresh perm with a new colour, a rebonding, or a keratin on the same lengths is the overlap the consultation guide already flags as a common cause of breakage. If you had any of those recently, it should have been said before the perm, not discovered in week two.

Humidity is the maintenance problem

Singapore air will relax a curl that is left wet, tied up tight, or dried on the bus. Once you are allowed to style it, dry the roots and let the ends fall the way the stylist showed you, instead of pinning the whole head up while it is damp. A tight tie on the day the hair is still settling is how a curl collapses at the crown. If the hair feels elastic and does not spring back, stop adding heat and message the outlet. That is the same elasticity check the salon uses before it will do another chemical service.

Which service you actually had

Rebonding and keratin are the smooth options. A digital perm is the wave. If the result looks straighter than you asked for, or curlier, the comparison guide is the page that separates them, and the outlet can look at it in person. Home care does not change a service into a different one.
