Using CDC Vouchers At The Salon

Apr 22, 2026 2 min read

On Hair Salon & Beauty accepts CDC Vouchers for eligible services. To keep checkout smooth, it helps to prepare your voucher QR code before payment and confirm eligibility with our team.

Before Your Visit

If you plan to use CDC Vouchers, mention it when booking or when you arrive. This lets the team confirm whether your intended service is eligible before the appointment starts.

At Checkout

Open your voucher QR code and show it to the team at payment. If your bill includes items that are not eligible, we will explain the remaining balance clearly.

Check Current Terms

CDC Voucher terms can change, so eligibility is always subject to the current programme guidelines and outlet acceptance rules at the time of payment.

Final Thoughts

Using CDC Vouchers is straightforward when the QR code is ready and eligibility is checked before payment.
