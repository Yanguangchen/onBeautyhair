Using CDC Vouchers at the Salon

Apr 22, 2026 Updated Sep 28, 2026 4 min read

By the On Hair Salon & Beauty team

The short answer

Yes, On Hair Salon & Beauty accepts CDC Vouchers for eligible services. Tell the outlet you plan to use them when you book, open your voucher link on your phone at checkout, choose the amount to spend and let the team scan your QR code. Pay any remaining balance in cash. You never need to share your Singpass details or any one-time password with us.

CDC Vouchers were designed for exactly the kind of place we are: neighbourhood businesses in the heartlands. A fresh haircut, a colour touch-up or a quick gelish manicure is a practical way to use them, and the process takes about as long as paying with your phone.

Here's how to make checkout smooth, avoid the usual snags, and keep your vouchers safe.

Before You Leave Home

  • Check your balance and validity on the official CDC Vouchers website. Amounts and expiry dates change with each tranche, so the official site is the one source of truth.
  • Find your voucher link, usually the SMS you received when you claimed. Keep it easy to reach on your phone.
  • Charge your phone and check your data. The QR code loads online, so a flat battery or no signal is the most common checkout delay.
  • Mention it when you book. Tell the outlet you'd like to pay with CDC Vouchers so they can confirm your planned service is eligible.

At Checkout, Step by Step

  1. Let us know you're paying with vouchers. The team will confirm the total and which part of the bill the vouchers can cover.
  2. Open your voucher link. Your available vouchers appear on screen.
  3. Choose the amount to spend. Select vouchers that add up as close to your bill as possible.
  4. Show the QR code. The team scans it, and the vouchers are deducted from your balance.
  5. Settle any balance. Pay the rest in cash, and you're done.

Paying the Balance

CDC Vouchers come in fixed denominations, and the scheme doesn't give change on vouchers. If your bill doesn't match your vouchers exactly, spend the vouchers that fit best and pay the difference in cash.

Tip: services with a "+" or "++" price, like colour or rebonding, are confirmed after consultation. Ask for the final price before we start, so you know exactly how much to cover with vouchers and how much in cash.

Stay Scam-Safe

Important: to accept your vouchers, our team only needs to scan the QR code on your screen. We will never ask for your Singpass login, password or any one-time password (OTP). Claim and check vouchers only through official government channels, and be wary of messages asking you to "verify" or "claim" vouchers through unfamiliar links.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use CDC Vouchers for a haircut?

CDC Vouchers are accepted for eligible services. Let the outlet know what you plan to book and the team will confirm eligibility before your service starts.

Will I get change if my vouchers are more than the bill?

No change is given on CDC Vouchers, so choose vouchers that add up as close to your bill as possible and pay any difference in cash.

Can I pay partly with vouchers and partly with cash?

Yes. Use your vouchers for part of the bill and pay the remaining balance in cash.

Do I need Singpass at the salon?

No. You only need to open your voucher link and show the QR code for our team to scan. Never share your Singpass details or OTPs with anyone.

Final Thoughts

Paying with CDC Vouchers is quick when your link is ready, your phone is charged and eligibility is checked before the service starts. Plan around the price list, keep a little cash for the balance, and enjoy the refresh.
