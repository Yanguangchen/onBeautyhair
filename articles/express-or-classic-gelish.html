Express or Classic Gelish at the Salon

Oct 26, 2026 5 min read

By the On Hair Salon & Beauty team

The short answer

Express gelish manicure is S$18 and classic gelish manicure is S$38. Express pedicure is S$20 and classic pedicure is S$58. Nail extension is S$108. The final chair is confirmed with the outlet you book.

The published gelish lines

  • Express gelish manicure: S$18. Express gelish pedicure: S$20. Both together: S$33.
  • Classic gelish manicure: S$38. Classic gelish pedicure: S$58. Both together: S$88.
  • Nail extension: S$108.

These figures are the ones printed on the services page. Prices marked with a plus elsewhere on the menu are starting prices and are confirmed after a consultation. The gelish lines above are listed as set amounts. If you are adding nail art or a repair, ask the outlet for the total before you sit down.

Express or classic

Express is the shorter, lower-priced gelish visit. Classic is the higher-priced one. The page does not print a minute count for either, so this guide will not invent how long you will be in the chair. Tell the outlet whether you want hands, feet, or both, and whether a nail is broken.

An extension is a different service at S$108. Book it by that name if you want length added, rather than assuming classic gelish includes it.

Where to book

On Hair Salon & Beauty has outlets across Singapore, including Toa Payoh, Simei, Bukit Merah, Clementi, Yishun, Ang Mo Kio, Bedok, and Serangoon. Message the outlet you will actually visit. CDC Vouchers are accepted for eligible services. Hair colour, rebonding, and keratin are separate menus and have their own guides.
