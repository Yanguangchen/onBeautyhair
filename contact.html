Sister Brand

Part of the same beauty group

ON Hair & Beauty is part of the same beauty group as K Beauty. Our salon focuses on hair services such as haircut, colouring, treatment, and styling, while K Beauty focuses on facial, lash, brow, and beauty treatments. Both brands are located side by side, giving customers a convenient one stop hair and beauty experience.

Looking for beauty, facial, lash, or brow services? Visit the K Beauty website.

For easier customer discovery, contact, booking, and reviews, our Google presence may be managed through one shared official Google Business Profile.

Both brands are located side by side. Customers may find our shared Google listing, reviews, and directions under our official group presence.
