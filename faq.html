Booking & Outlets

Do I need to make an appointment?

Appointments are recommended, especially for colour, rebonding, perms, treatments, brow and eyeliner work, and weekend slots, because these services take longer. Walk-ins may be accepted when the team has availability, so a quick WhatsApp to the outlet before you head over can save you a wait.

How do I book an appointment?

Message or call the outlet you want to visit. Every outlet's phone and WhatsApp numbers are on our contact page. For the fastest reply, include the service you want, your preferred date and time, and a short note on your hair history, such as recent colour, bleaching or rebonding.

Which outlet should I contact?

Contact the outlet you plan to visit, since each outlet manages its own bookings. You'll find the address, phone number, WhatsApp link, opening hours and map for every outlet on our contact page.

Where are your outlets?

We have ten neighbourhood outlets: Toa Payoh (Lorong 1), Simei Eastpoint Mall, Simei Street 3, Bukit Merah Block 2, 163 Bukit Merah Central, Clementi, Yishun Ring Road, Ang Mo Kio Avenue 10, Bedok North Street 3 and Serangoon Central Drive. Full addresses and directions are on the contact page.

What are your opening hours?

Most outlets are open daily from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Bukit Merah Block 2 is open daily from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Serangoon Central Drive is open from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekends. Hours may vary on public holidays, so check with the outlet before a holiday visit.

Can I request a specific stylist?

Yes, share your preference when booking. Availability depends on the outlet, date, time and how long your service takes, so booking a little ahead gives you the best chance of seeing the stylist you want.

What if I am running late?

Contact the outlet as soon as possible. The team will let you know whether your appointment can still go ahead or needs to be adjusted, since longer services like colour or rebonding can run into the next booking.

Can I reschedule or cancel an appointment?

Yes. Let the outlet know as early as you can by WhatsApp or phone. Early notice helps the team offer your slot to someone else and find you a new time that works.

Do your teams speak Mandarin?

Our outlets serve customers in English and Mandarin. If you're more comfortable in another language, mention it when you book and the team will do their best to help.

Are ON Hair Salon & Beauty and K Beauty related?

Yes. ON Hair Salon & Beauty is part of the same beauty group as K Beauty. We focus on hair services such as haircuts, colouring, treatments and styling, while K Beauty focuses on facial, lash, brow and beauty treatments. The two brands sit side by side at Simei Street 3, Bukit Merah Block 2 and Serangoon Central Drive. Visit the K Beauty website for their services.

What do "+" and "++" mean on the price list?

A "+" or "++" means the listed amount is a starting price. The final price depends on things like hair length, thickness, condition and the amount of product needed, and the team confirms it with you after consultation.

Hair Services

Can I get a consultation before booking a service?

Yes. A consultation is recommended for colour changes, corrective work, bridal styling and treatments where the right approach depends on your current hair or skin condition. Read how our consultation works to know what to expect.

What should I prepare before a hair colour appointment?

Bring reference photos and share any previous chemical services, box dye, bleaching or treatments. This helps the stylist plan a result that suits your hair history. See our colour appointment preparation guide for the full checklist.

Can you fix a box dye or colour from another salon?

Often, yes, but corrective colour is rarely a one-step job. Hair that has been box-dyed, especially in dark or black shades, may need to be lightened gradually over more than one session to protect its condition. Bring photos of your goal and be upfront about every product used, so your stylist can plan a realistic route.

What is the difference between rebonding and keratin treatment?

Rebonding permanently straightens the treated hair by reshaping its internal bonds, so it stays straight until it grows out. Keratin treatment smooths frizz and softens the hair while keeping more natural movement, and it gradually fades over the following months. Our rebonding vs keratin vs digital perm guide compares them in detail.

What is the difference between a perm and a digital perm?

Both create curls or waves. A regular perm sets the pattern with a chemical solution alone and tends to give a springier curl that looks most defined when wet. A digital perm uses heated rods to create softer, looser waves that look most defined when dry. Your stylist can recommend which suits your hair and styling routine.

How long do colour, perm and rebonding appointments take?

It depends on your hair length, thickness and the technique. As a rough guide, a single-process colour can take around 1.5 to 2 hours, while highlights, perms, digital perms and rebonding can take 3 hours or more. Tell the outlet your hair length when booking and they can give you a better estimate.

Can I colour and rebond on the same day?

It depends on your hair's condition. Combining chemical services in one visit places extra stress on the hair, so stylists often prefer to space them out, commonly rebonding first and colouring later. Your stylist will check your hair during consultation and advise the safest order.

Do you offer hair and scalp treatments?

Yes. Hair treatments and scalp care are part of our services, our menu includes Keratin Treatment, and our perm and rebonding packages come with treatment support. If your hair feels dry, frizzy or weak, read when hair needs treatment, not just a trim, then ask your stylist to check your hair and scalp.

How long does eyebrow embroidery last?

Semi-permanent brow work usually lasts around one to three years, depending on your skin type, skincare routine and sun exposure. Oily skin and frequent exfoliation tend to fade pigment faster. Your artist will advise whether and when a touch-up makes sense.

What is the difference between eyelash extensions and an eyelash lift?

Eyelash extensions add individual lashes to your own for extra length and fullness. Our Eyelash Extension is S$58 and Style Eyelash Extension is S$88. An Eyelash Lift (S$38) curls and lifts your natural lashes without adding any, for a more natural, low-maintenance look.

Aftercare

How long should I wait to wash my hair after rebonding or a perm?

Stylists commonly recommend waiting about 48 to 72 hours before washing, tying or clipping your hair after rebonding or a perm, so the new shape can set. Your stylist will give you the exact timing for your service.

How do I make my hair colour last longer in Singapore's humidity?

Wash with a colour-safe shampoo, use lukewarm rather than hot water, protect your hair from strong sun and heat tools, and keep up regular conditioning or treatments. Warm, natural shades also tend to fade more gracefully than cool ash or pastel tones. Our colour guide covers the full routine.

How should I care for my brows after embroidery?

Keep the area clean and dry, avoid picking or rubbing as it heals, skip makeup on the brows, and stay away from saunas, swimming pools and strong sun until your artist says the skin has healed. Follow the aftercare instructions your artist gives you, as they're tailored to your treatment.

Do you provide aftercare advice?

Yes. Aftercare varies by service, so ask your stylist what to use, what to avoid and when to schedule maintenance. You're also welcome to message the outlet if anything feels off after your visit.
