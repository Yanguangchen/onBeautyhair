Appointments are recommended, especially for color, treatments, bridal services and weekend slots. Walk-ins may be accepted when the team has availability.

Can I get a consultation before booking a service?

Yes. A consultation is recommended for color changes, corrective work, bridal styling and treatments where the right approach depends on your current hair or skin condition.

Are prices fixed?

Some services depend on hair length, thickness, condition, product use or the complexity of the requested look. The team can confirm the final price before starting.

What should I prepare before a hair color appointment?

Bring reference photos and share any previous chemical services, box dye, bleaching or treatments. This helps the stylist plan a result that suits your hair history.

Can I request a specific stylist?

You can share your preference when booking. Availability depends on the selected outlet, date, time and service duration.

What if I am running late?

Contact the outlet as soon as possible. The team will advise whether your appointment can still proceed or needs to be adjusted.

Do you provide aftercare advice?

Yes. Aftercare recommendations can vary by service, so ask your stylist what to use, what to avoid and when to schedule maintenance.
